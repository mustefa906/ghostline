## Accomplishments that we're proud of

A working product in a day, built spec first. A founder records a voice note, Whisper transcribes it, the app builds a voice fingerprint from three to five of their past posts, and an interview agent asks the follow-ups a ghostwriter would ask, three at most, one at a time. Then it drafts a LinkedIn post only from what the founder said, with a source note on every paragraph, and the founder edits it, schedules it, and copies it out when it is due.

The part we are proudest of is the rule holding. The draft never invents a missing detail. The gap stays visible, and the sources are shown. The spec, the steering docs, and the copy hook all ship in the public repo, and 21 tests pass with a green production build. A demo mode seeds a made-up founder so anyone can try the full flow in a minute.

## What we learned

Writing the spec before the code changed how the build went. The requirements caught the edge cases early, and the steering docs kept every later decision honest to the first rule. I also learned that the product is the interview, not the draft. Anyone can generate a post. The value is in asking the right question at the right moment.

## What's next for Ghostline

A live voice interview agent, so the founder talks instead of typing, and posting straight to LinkedIn from the queue. After that, the same interview for teams, so a whole founding team can keep one voice across their posts.
