# Ghostline - Product Steering

## What Ghostline is

Ghostline turns a founder's spoken thinking into LinkedIn posts that sound
like them. Talk for two minutes, answer a few questions, get a draft you can
stand behind. It is the founder's own ghostwriting process, productized.

## The one rule

**Interview first. Never write from a prompt alone.** A draft may use only:
the voice-note transcript, the interview answers, and the voice fingerprint.
If a detail is missing, leave it out. Never invent proof, numbers, or quotes.

## House writing rules (apply to every draft the app generates)

- Kind, gentle, raw tone.
- Simple hyphens only. Never em dashes.
- No "X, not Y" constructions. Say the thing itself, plainly.
- Short spaced lines, one beat at a time. Skip a line after every sentence.
- Problem before product. Recognition over vague claims. Outcome over mechanism.
- The founder is the protagonist, not the product.

## What Ghostline is not

- Not an AI writer that turns a one-line prompt into a post.
- Not a scheduler that auto-posts to LinkedIn (copy-to-clipboard only in the MVP).
- Not a content farm. One idea, one post, done well.
